Add the Download button to YouTube (one time, about 2 minutes)

You can already download by pasting links into the Video Downloader window. These steps add a handy Download button right under every YouTube video.

  1. Install the free Tampermonkey extension It lets your browser run small helper scripts like this one. Pick your browser:
    Chrome / Brave Edge Firefox Opera / Opera GX
  2. Let Tampermonkey run scripts Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera ask for this once. Right-click the Tampermonkey icon (top right, maybe under the puzzle-piece icon) → Manage extension and turn on Allow User Scripts. If you don't see that switch, turn on Developer mode on the extensions page instead. Firefox needs nothing here.
  3. Add the button script Click Install the button. Tampermonkey opens its install page: click Install. Installed this way, the button also keeps itself up to date.
    Install the button
    If that doesn't open Tampermonkey: click the Tampermonkey icon → Create a new script, select everything in the editor (Ctrl+A), paste the script (Ctrl+V) and save (Ctrl+S). Setup already put it on your clipboard, or copy it here:
    Copied!
  4. Try it Open any YouTube video (refresh the page if it was already open). A red Download button appears under the video. The first time you click it your browser asks to open Video Downloader: tick Always allow and click Open.

How to use it

From YouTube

Click Download to download with the option that has the ✓. Click the small arrow next to it to choose another option. Shortcuts: Shift+D downloads, Shift+C opens Clip to save just part of a video. Click as many videos as you like - they wait in line and download one after another.

From the Video Downloader window

Open Video Downloader from the Start menu. Copy a link (from YouTube or most other video sites), and it is filled in for you - pick a type and press Download. The window shows progress, lets you Pause and Resume, and lists everything you downloaded (double-click a finished one to find the file).

Pausing, turning off the PC, losing Wi-Fi

Nothing is lost. Half-finished downloads continue from where they stopped the next time you open Video Downloader, click Resume, or download something else.

Getting videos onto your phone

Use the Phone option. Its videos are saved to a folder your phone can see:

  • iPhone with iCloud for Windows: they go to iCloud Drive\Phone Videos automatically. On the iPhone open the Files app → iCloud Drive → Phone Videos. To put one in Photos, open it, tap Share → Save Video.
  • OneDrive or Google Drive: in Settings → Phone, choose a folder inside OneDrive / Google Drive, then open that app on your phone.
  • No cloud: they go to Downloads\Phone. Copy them over with a USB cable, or send them to yourself.

Your watch history, sorted by length

Click Watch history in the Video Downloader window. It lists the videos you've watched with their length, so you can sort by longest (the default), search, filter (over 1 hour, Shorts only...), save the list as a spreadsheet, or select videos and download them.

Quick: get it from YouTube

Click Get from YouTube. This reads your recent history (usually the last few thousand videos) and needs your YouTube sign-in file - the same one used for age-restricted videos.

Everything, back to the start: import a Google Takeout export

  1. Open Google Takeout Go to takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Choose only your YouTube history Click Deselect all, then tick YouTube and YouTube Music. Click All YouTube data included, click Deselect all again and tick only history. (Optional: under Multiple formats set History to JSON - HTML works too.)
  3. Create the export Click Next step → Create export. Google emails you a download link, usually within minutes.
  4. Import it Download the .zip, then in Watch history click Import Google Takeout file... and pick the .zip (no need to unzip it).

Takeout files don't include video lengths, so Video Downloader looks them up afterwards - a few thousand videos take roughly 10-30 minutes. You can close the window meanwhile; click Stop to pause it and Find missing lengths to carry on.

Good to know: the length is the whole video's length, not how long you watched it (YouTube doesn't share that). Videos that were removed show "-". Your history stays on this PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\VideoDownloader\watch-history.tsv; Clear list deletes that copy and never touches your YouTube account.

Age-restricted and members-only videos

YouTube only shows these to signed-in accounts, so the downloader needs your YouTube sign-in. It is only used when a video won't download without it.

Using Firefox? There's an easier way: stay signed in to YouTube in Firefox, then in Video Downloader open Settings → Network and set Where your sign-in comes from to Firefox. Nothing to export. (Chrome, Edge and Opera lock their sign-ins so other programs can't read them, so for those use the steps below.)

  1. Install "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" (a free, open-source extension) from your browser's extension store.
  2. Export your YouTube sign-in from a private window Open a new private / incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N; allow the extension in private windows if asked), sign in to youtube.com there, click the extension and click Export. Then close the private window without signing out. (If you export from your normal window, YouTube quietly replaces the sign-in as soon as you keep browsing, and the file stops working.)
  3. Give it to Video Downloader Rename it to cookies.txt and move it into this folder:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\VideoDownloader Copied!
    (Paste the path into the File Explorer address bar to open the folder.)

Video Downloader then encrypts it and deletes the plain file (Windows ties the encryption to your account, so a copy of it is useless on any other PC or account). It is only ever sent to YouTube.

Keep cookies.txt private. It works like a password for your YouTube/Google account. Never send it to anyone, and don't leave a copy in Downloads. If YouTube signs you out or the sign-in stops working, export a fresh one the same way. To stop using it, delete cookies.dpapi from that folder.

Problems?

There's no Download button on YouTube
  • Refresh the YouTube page.
  • Check that Tampermonkey shows the script as enabled (Tampermonkey icon → Dashboard).
  • Chrome / Edge / Opera: make sure Allow User Scripts (or Developer mode) is on - see step 2 above.
  • Long videos: the button sits next to Like/Share. If YouTube has no room there it moves under the title, or to the bottom-right corner.
  • Still nothing? Every release on the project page also has the button as a browser extension (no Tampermonkey needed) - see the README there.
I click Download but nothing happens

Your browser may have blocked the "Open Video Downloader?" question. Look for a small icon in the address bar, or run Setup again to reconnect the button. You can always paste the link into the Video Downloader window instead.

A download failed

Open Video Downloader and double-click the red line to see why. Common reasons: the video is private or removed; it is age-restricted (see above); or YouTube changed something - the downloader updates itself and retries automatically. If it keeps failing, click Help → Check for updates now in the window.

"YouTube is blocking downloads" or "Waiting for the internet"

Your downloads are safe in the queue. Turn off any VPN, check your Wi-Fi, wait a bit (YouTube's block usually lifts within an hour) and press Resume.

"Your network is blocking secure connections" (self-signed certificate)

Something between your PC and the internet is answering secure (https) connections with its own certificate instead of YouTube's. This usually comes from a router or ISP filter, parental controls, a work/school network, or security software that "scans" encrypted traffic. It can also break other apps that check certificates strictly. Your downloads stay in the queue; they continue on a connection that isn't filtered, or once whoever manages the network or security software turns that filtering off for this PC.

Downloads are slow

Usually it's the connection: VPNs, a busy network, or YouTube limiting speed. In Settings → Network, set Parallel download pieces to 8. To see what's going on, click Help → Check my connection: after about two minutes a report opens with your Wi-Fi signal, band, ping times and a speed test.

Windows said "Windows protected your PC" when I ran Setup

That appears for any program that isn't from the Microsoft Store. Click More info → Run anyway. Video Downloader is a plain PowerShell script you can read in Notepad.

Where are the log files?

Click Help → Open the data folder (it's %LOCALAPPDATA%\VideoDownloader): history.log (every download), runner.log (the last downloads in detail) and error.log. Logs are trimmed automatically, and connection reports are deleted after 30 days.

Privacy: who can see what I download?

Everything Video Downloader keeps (the list of downloads, logs, your sign-in and watch history) is in a folder only your Windows account can open; your sign-in is also encrypted so a copied file is useless on another account or PC. Notifications say "Download finished" without the video's name. Clear finished removes finished downloads from the list and the log that names them; Help → Erase my download history deletes the list, all logs, the connection reports and the playlist memory in one go; and Settings → General → Forget finished downloads after does it automatically after 1, 7, 30 or 90 days. Your downloaded files themselves are never touched - delete those like any other file. Anyone who can sign in to your Windows account (or is an administrator of the PC) can still see your files, so use a password on your account.

How do I update Video Downloader?

When a new version is out, a blue "Version ... is out - update" link appears at the top right of the window. Click it and choose Yes: it downloads the new version, checks it against its published checksum, installs it and reopens the window. Your settings and downloads are kept. yt-dlp and Deno (the parts that talk to YouTube) update themselves every week; Help → Check for updates now does it straight away.

Uninstall

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Video Downloader → Uninstall. Your downloaded videos are kept. Then delete the script from Tampermonkey's dashboard.
